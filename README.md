# IEOR_4571_Group_Project
We examine the efficacy of quantitative trading strategies in capturing market trends and identifying potential price reversals. By evaluating their historical performance against conventional passive investment approaches, we seek to determine whether systematic trading signals can meaningfully improve investment outcomes on both an absolute and risk-adjusted basis.

## Data
The paper leverages daily prices for the iShares’s iBoxx $ Investment Grade Corporate Bond ETF (LQD). The ETF is among the largest and most liquid, providing diversified exposure to USD denominated investment-grade corporate bonds. Launched in July 2002, the fund manages approximately $33 billion in assets as of November 2026, ranking third among passive U.S.-listed corporate bond ETFs by assets under management.
